# NBA late-swap portfolio caps and evidence

Simulated late swap previously picked each entry independently using raw Top 1% minus an appearance-count penalty. It did not invoke the initial builder's hard NBA exposure policy. The new grouped selector jointly chooses whole legal rosters with one quota per exact locked-slot pattern, canonical roster uniqueness across groups, and the existing ownership/minutes/overall cap policy. Integer incumbents are independently checked before use. Failed or timed-out searches do not relax caps or produce a partial export.

Already-started players cannot be removed. If uploaded entries already exceed a newly computed cap for a locked player, the effective maximum is exactly that inherited locked count. The UI and saved evidence identify the player, configured maximum and inherited count. This exception cannot add a started player. Positive fractional limits use the existing NBA export validator's minimum-one-entry rounding; zero limits and fades remain zero for open players.

The score is normalized to 0–1. A bounded same-group improvement pass uses the initial selector's marginal concentration and maximum-overlap scales. This is a constrained quality/diversity heuristic, not an exact joint-payout optimizer or proof of better future returns. Fast mode jointly reconsiders its generated proposals and legal uploaded originals; its narrower pool may fail even when a larger search could succeed.

Every export, including fast and automatic mode and post-tipoff reconciliation, independently checks the final portfolio exposure counts and canonical uniqueness. If restoring newly locked originals breaks the portfolio's caps, export is blocked until a new solve. Only unavoidable fully locked duplicate entries retain their existing multiplicity.

Successful manual and automatic runs queue a separate audit ZIP through the existing private archive before any export click and expose DOWNLOAD LATE SWAP AUDIT. It includes aligned candidate IDs and scores, candidate-group and entry mappings, selected candidate IDs, uploaded originals, input/lock snapshots, selection limits and exceptions, settings, and source files with SHA-256 hashes. Simulated runs additionally retain the frozen field, payouts, supplied final actuals, and the saved pregame pool/markets used for started-game uncertainty. Existing downloaded-CSV checkpoints retain the final reconciled result, including automatic exports. Storage still depends on the existing browser/private archive configuration and its success; this change cannot reconstruct earlier missing pools.

Validation covers joint group quotas, shared-player caps, fades/minimums, infeasible limits, inherited locks, fully locked duplicates, export mutation checks, metric-unit normalization, archive hash/codec round trips, and end-to-end simulated selections on DK's eight and FD's nine roster slots. Existing late-swap, lock refresh, auto-swap, ownership-cap, and candidate tests also run. No completed-game results are used to choose the new policy.
