# Published NFL player outcomes

These records preserve explicit player FPTS from user-uploaded completed contest standings, separately from anonymous opponent-field profiles. They contain no entry IDs or usernames. The source file SHA-256, scoring platform and slate date are recorded in each snapshot.

The WAS at SF FanDuel snapshot contains 49 published player scores, including kickers, DST and explicit zeros. Unlisted players remain unknown. Source final status is asserted by the uploaded completed standings, not independently verified here. Posted scores may be corrected by the contest provider.

These files load into the player-result validation archive. They do not refit projections, alter simulation parameters, or establish a persistent edge. Future projection validation must use correctly matched frozen pregame inputs; outcomes must never overwrite those predictions.
